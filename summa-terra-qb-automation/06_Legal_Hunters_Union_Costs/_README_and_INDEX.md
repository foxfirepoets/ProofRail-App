# Hunters Landing & Union Walk — Completion-Cost Document Index & Summary
### Prepared for STV's legal counsel | Construction dispute: Makers Line (fired GC) vs. completion by Rich Development

**Date assembled:** 2026-06-25
**Prepared by:** Summa Terra Ventures (document compilation)
**Scope:** This package gathers the financial documents that show (a) what the original general contractor **Makers Line ("ML")** was contracted/paid before it was terminated, and (b) what STV ultimately paid **Rich Development ("RD")** and subcontractors to finish each project after ML was fired for installing incorrect materials.

> **IMPORTANT — read this first.** The dollar figures below are transcribed from STV's own internal accounting spreadsheets, partner cash-call memos, pay applications, and change-order logs. They are management/working figures, not audited. Several numbers come from working drafts that were revised over time, so different documents show different totals for the same line. Where a number could NOT be confirmed from the documents in this package, it is flagged **[GAP]**. Counsel should treat the flagged items as open questions to confirm with the client and against the underlying pay-applications/bank records before relying on them.

> **Plain-English bottom line for Hunters Landing:** STV paid Rich Development roughly **$13.48M** to take over and finish the project after Makers Line was fired. RD's revised contract (after change orders, contract contributions, and a landscape allowance overage) came to about **$13.83M**, so the analysis describes a net **concession of ~$600k** in STV's favor. On TOP of the RD contract, STV incurred: the **40 approved RD change orders ($524,771.75)**, the **exterior rebuild "budget to fix" scope (~$2.69M scheduled / ~$2.24M billed, much of it to redo ML's defective materials)**, **unpaid-sub invoices left by ML**, and an additional **~$1.0M "funds required"** to close out per the Final Draw Analysis. The exterior rebuild and the "out of scope / done under ML" items (~$704k) are the costs most directly attributable to Makers Line's incorrect materials.

> **[GAP] The single clean number "X dollars of additional cost caused specifically by Makers Line" is NOT stated anywhere in this package.** It has to be built up from: (rebuild-to-fix scope) + (unpaid-sub invoices after ML) + (ML-attributable change orders) − (insurance recovery $105,417 / $49,944.09) − (contract contributions). Counsel should have the client produce or confirm that single causation figure; the documents here supply the components but not the total.
